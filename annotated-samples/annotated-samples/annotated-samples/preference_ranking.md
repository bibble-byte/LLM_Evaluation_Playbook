# Preference Ranking Evaluation

## Task Type
Pairwise comparison of two model responses to determine alignment with human preferences.

## Prompt
"Explain the importance of regular exercise to an elderly person."

## Response A
Regular exercise helps maintain mobility, improves heart health, and supports mental well-being.

## Response B
Exercise is good for everyone and should be done daily.

## Preferred Response
Response A

## Evaluation Criteria
- Clarity
- Completeness
- User-appropriate tone
- Helpfulness

## Rationale
Response A provides specific, age-appropriate benefits, while Response B is generic and less informative.
