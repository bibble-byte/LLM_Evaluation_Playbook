# Hallucination Detection Evaluation

## Task Type
Detection of fabricated or unsupported claims in LLM-generated responses.

## Prompt
"Explain the side effects of a newly approved drug for diabetes."

## Model Response (Sample)
The drug improves insulin sensitivity and also cures type 1 diabetes completely.

## Hallucination Identified
- Claim that the drug cures type 1 diabetes (unsupported and false)

## Evaluation Criteria
- Factual correctness
- Source grounding
- Risk severity

## Score
2 / 5

## Rationale
The response contains a critical hallucination by claiming a complete cure, which is medically inaccurate and potentially harmful.
